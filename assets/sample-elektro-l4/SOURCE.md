# Elektro-L4 RDAS sample (preview)

Real MSU-GS VIS channels from an **Elektro-L4 RDAS** acquisition, publicly shared
for calibration in [Digitelektro/rdas_layer_aligner issue #2](https://github.com/Digitelektro/rdas_layer_aligner/issues/2)
(author: eswnl), decoded from the CADU with SatDump (`elektro_rdas` pipeline).

These files are **downscaled 8-bit previews** (~800 px wide, auto-levelled) for
demonstration only — not full-resolution products and not radiometrically
calibrated. Full processing requires full-resolution products decoded with
SatDump. The underlying satellite imagery is © its operator (Roscosmos /
NTs OMZ); redistributed here only as a small low-resolution preview sample.
